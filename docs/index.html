Canal Studio

A private Windows desktop app for producing documentary videos for the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • The owner chooses topics. The app drafts a script with AI and fact-checks it.
  • The owner reviews, edits and approves every script.
  • The app records the narration, builds the visuals and renders the video locally.
  • The owner watches and approves the final video.
  • Only then does the app upload it to the owner's channel, with the "altered or synthetic content" disclosure turned on.

The app runs only on the owner's computer. It has no public users and no backend server.
