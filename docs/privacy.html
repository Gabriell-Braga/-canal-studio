Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Canal Studio is a Windows desktop application. It helps its owner produce documentary videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is a private tool, not a public service: it has no website accounts, no backend server and no users other than the channel owner.

Google and YouTube data the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, the app asks for these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload the videos you approved.
  • youtube.force-ssl: to set the custom thumbnail and upload the English subtitles of those videos.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel name and picture.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to show views, watch time, average view duration and subscribers gained of the videos the app uploaded.

How the data is used

  • Only to upload your approved videos and show your own channel statistics to you.
  • Never for advertising, profiling, selling or training AI models.
  • YouTube and Google data is not sent to any other service. It goes only between your computer and Google's APIs.

Where the data is stored

  • Everything stays on your own computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted with Windows data protection (DPAPI) and stored in the app's local database.
  • Channel statistics are cached in the same local database to show them offline.
  • The developer has no server and receives no copy of your data.

Other services

To write scripts the app can use Anthropic's Claude API or a local model. Only the video topic and the script text are sent there, never YouTube data. Stock footage is searched on Pixabay, Pexels and Wikimedia Commons with keywords from the script.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • In the app, click Desconectar on the YouTube screen to delete the stored tokens.
  • You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored.

YouTube Terms

By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
